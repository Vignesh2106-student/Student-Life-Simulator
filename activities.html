Survival notes

Small choices.
Big semester.

There is no perfect calendar—but the strongest students plan for the rough days before they arrive.

01

Don’t ignore sleep.

Energy powers nearly everything. Recover before your student burns out.

02

Protect your health.

Keep health above critical levels, especially when the calendar gets busy.

03

Mix focus and friends.

Academics matter, but social time and happiness make high effort sustainable.

04

Save for surprises.

A little cash buffer keeps an unexpected expense from ruining a great week.

05

Happiness is fuel.

Happy students handle challenges better. Schedule genuine joy, not just work.

06

Rest strategically.

When energy dips, choose sleep or relaxation before trying to power through.
